# College Bus Tracking System - Storyboard

## Step 1 - Project Planning
Identify the requirements and features of the system.

## Step 2 - Login
Create a login page for students and administrators.

## Step 3 - Student Management
Add and manage student details.

## Step 4 - Bus Management
Add bus number, capacity and other bus details.

## Step 5 - Driver Management
Add driver name, contact details and assigned bus.

## Step 6 - Route Management
Add bus routes and stops.

## Step 7 - Live Bus Tracking
Display the current location of the bus.

## Step 8 - Notifications
Send notifications when the bus approaches a stop.

## Step 9 - Testing
Test all the features of the system.

## Step 10 - Final Project
Complete the system and update the documentation.
